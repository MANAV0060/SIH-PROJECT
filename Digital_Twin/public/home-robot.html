AERIS-TWIN uses a galvanically isolated, high-impedance CAN bus sniffer. Because it never sends write packets or interrupts the bus clock, it cannot interfere with primary flight control. Under DO-178C software-first guidelines, this qualifies as a non-intrusive advisory companion system.

Pure black-box machine learning can hallucinate catastrophic predictions when encountering edge-case flight regimes (e.g. rapid altitude drops or extreme desert heat). By enforcing conservation of energy and Navier-Stokes thermodynamic bounds directly in the loss function, PINNs remain physically bounded and reliable.
